Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Who we are

Clearline Web builds websites for small businesses across Warwickshire and the West Midlands. You can reach us at hello@clearlineweb.co.uk. We're the data controller for the personal information described in this policy.

What we collect, and why

Contact form. When you use the form on our homepage, we collect your name, business name, email address and message. Submitting the form opens a pre-filled email addressed to hello@clearlineweb.co.uk from your own email client — it's sent directly from your device, not processed through any third-party form service.

Live chat. If you use the chat widget on this site, your conversation is handled by Tawk.to, our live chat provider, so we can reply to you. Tawk.to may also collect your IP address, browser type, and the pages you've visited, in order to run the chat service.

We don't run ads on this site, we don't sell your data, and we don't use analytics or tracking cookies.

Cookies

The only non-essential cookie-setting feature on this site is the live chat widget (Tawk.to). It only loads once you accept cookies via the banner shown on your first visit — if you choose "Reject," the chat widget won't load, and you can still reach us via the contact form or email. You can change your choice at any time using the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer.

Where your data goes

Tawk.to is based in the United States and is certified under the UK Extension to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, a recognised safeguard for transferring personal data there.

How long we keep it

Enquiries sent via the contact form go straight to our inbox and are kept only as long as needed to handle your enquiry. Chat records via Tawk.to are kept on a similar basis, after which they're deleted.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you can ask what personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and object to how we use it. To exercise any of these, email hello@clearlineweb.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you're unhappy with how we've handled your data.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: hello@clearlineweb.co.uk.
